# Kosplora: the open university

*A complete, organized list of every educational resource in the Kosplora corpus. Generated from `kosplora/lens.js` (the single source of truth); regenerate, do not hand-edit.*

Curiosity and creativity drive learning at every age. When exploring is engaging, through stories and visuals, an open, university-like education can assemble itself from the free web. This is one reader’s map of where that can start, offered so you can begin your own.

**About the two marks.** The small words beside each channel translate Bentley’s two rough calls: how steep it feels for a newcomer (the prerequisite-load criterion in open-education rubrics) and how much you learn per minute (the time-to-value criterion). Rough calls to start from, not measured scores. On the site, the doors are drawn straight from those marks; channels he left unmarked keep their place on the shelves, present, just not competing for the doors.

**Links.** Every link has been checked to resolve to a real destination. Channel links go straight to the channel where its exact page was title-confirmed; the remaining few point to a YouTube search for the channel name (a real, working search), never a guessed address that could be the wrong or an impersonating channel.

**Scale.** 101 channels across 17 subjects, plus 32 further resources (courses, open books, tools, references, data, and single works).

## Notes for the self-taught

> Some "stream anything" sites pay for themselves with malware and worse. A content blocker such as uBlock Origin is the seatbelt. Learning to move safely on the open web is part of the education, not a detour from it.
>
> Hunting a free copy of a good book means meeting sketchy ads and popups. That friction teaches you to see and act carefully in cyberspace.
>
> Opinion and error run through many of these channels. That is the point: see the range, check the sources, and let your own judgment grow. Exposure with a habit of verification beats a walled garden.
>

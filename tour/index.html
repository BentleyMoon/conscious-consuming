Step 4 of 4 · ActFig. 4. What the four of them would do, with the reason for each line.

Turn it into a plan

Their shared weights, applied to the same facts. What they split on counts as neutral, so nobody's strongest view quietly wins.

Devil's advocate. "Every weight within 2 of the others" is a generous rule for four friends. Forty people would split on almost everything, and a real group needs a better rule than this demo's. The group values tool shows the whole spread instead of one yes or no.

A plan is a suggestion the group chooses to commit to. It keeps the reason for each line and who disagreed. Make a shared plan.

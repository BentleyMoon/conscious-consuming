Relationship file

A relationship is a small sourced record

A contribution should be as inspectable as a fact correction: two stable endpoints, one relation, a source, and an as-of date when the claim can change.

Asserted edge Ownership, alternative, manufacturer, or organizer links need a source.
Computed edge Resemblance can be derived from value signatures and labelled as computed.
Combined relationship Relationship files dedupe by from + rel + to and keep the source trail.
Citable relationship Nodes can export as JSON-LD so the commons joins the wider web.

Have one? Follow that shape and Add a relationship. One clear change with its source beats ten vague ones.

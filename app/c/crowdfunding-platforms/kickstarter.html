Low platform and payment fees, more of what is given reaches the campaign: Strong. Kickstarter's own fees page states that if a project is successfully funded, it collects Kickstarter's 5% fee plus payment processing fees of between 3% and 5%: 3% + $0.30 per pledge for standard pledges, or a discounted micropledge fee of 5% + $0.08 per pledge for pledges under $10. If funding is not successful, there are no fees. source↗ 2026

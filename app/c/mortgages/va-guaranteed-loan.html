The VA states its home loan benefit generally requires no down payment and has no monthly mortgage insurance, but charges a one-time funding fee of 0.5 to 3.3 percent depending on loan type, down payment, and whether it is a first use of the benefit; veterans receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability, among other groups, are exempt from the fee. The benefit itself is restricted to veterans, certain active-duty and reserve service members, and some surviving spouses. source↗ 2026

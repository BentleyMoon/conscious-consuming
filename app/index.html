How are these scores made?

Each entry carries sourced facts. Food uses measures such as Nutri-Score, NOVA, Green-Score, and declared labels from Open Food Facts. Researched categories cite their assessments. Your controls set the trade-offs. The score combines the facts with those choices and moves toward a neutral 50 when evidence is missing. Missing facts are never guessed. A high total can still hide one weak point, so we show it. Nobody can pay to rank.

## Staying

### Places to stay | cadence: yearly
- Hotels | hold: State attorney general settlements over hidden resort fees, and government labor and ADA findings, are real but they measure who got caught rather than current conduct: they track a chain's size and visibility more than its behaviour, so an axis built only from them would have put unsued brands at the top of a column that reads as good. The honest primary measure is whether a chain's own booking flow shows the total price, mandatory fees included, at first display, which is checkable for every brand today rather than only the ones with a public record. It could not be gathered that way: the booking search or results page for ten of the twelve brands considered (Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, IHG, Choice, Motel 6, Red Roof Inn, Best Western, Extended Stay America, Radisson) returned an error to a plain fetch and to one with a full browser header, and only two (Wyndham, Omni) served a readable page at all, of which only Wyndham's said anything about fee inclusion. Held until that evidence can be gathered directly, in a browser, for most of the roster; the enforcement history remains worth keeping as a separate, clearly labelled record once the primary axis can carry its own weight.

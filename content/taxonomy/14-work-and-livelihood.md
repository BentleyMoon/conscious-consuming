edge: The decisions a person makes as a worker, and the small number a very small organisation makes on people's behalf. COICOP does not classify these, because they are not household consumption. They are on the map because where a person works and who represents them shapes more of a life than most purchases do, and because a catalogue that only addresses a shopper has decided in advance that a person is only a shopper.

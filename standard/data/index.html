The data

Every comparison in Conscious Consuming is an open data file you can download and score with your own app. There are 144, and every scored fact in them carries where it came from.

Licence

The data is under the Open Database License 1.0: you may copy, change and build on it, and you MUST share what you build from it under the same licence and say where it came from. Food and personal-care facts come from Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts under the same licence. The engine and the sites' code are under AGPL-3.0-or-later, and the guides under CC BY-SA 4.0.

How a fact is recorded

Every score sits beside a provenance record: a note saying what the fact is, the source it came from, and the date the source says it was true. A score with no source is not published; where the evidence is too thin, the measure is withheld. The record's fields are in the source format, and a whole file in the category dataset format.

The list of every file, with its criteria and counts, is index.json (its format).

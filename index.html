The method, in plain sight

A score is facts × your weights. Nothing hidden.

No black box, no proprietary ranking. Take the leader above: its number is the sourced facts, each multiplied by how much you said it matters, added up. Move a weight and you can watch exactly which fact moved the answer.

fact (sourced, 0–100)
  × your weight (the value trunk you set × its factor; shares below)   Σ ÷ total weight = score Facts we lack are pulled toward a neutral 50, never guessed. A high overall can still hide one weak axis, so the app flags it. Weighting one thing more means everything else counts a little less; that trade-off is the whole idea.

How it stays honest

The lines it will not cross

These are not features. They are the promise, and crossing any one of them makes it no longer this thing.

No ads, no paid rankYour values set the order. Nothing buys a spot at the top.
No account, no trackingYour values carry no name and stay on your device.
Every fact shows its source, or says it has noneA handful carry a note without a citation. Those are labelled, and never counted as independent sources.
ReproducibleSame inputs, same result. Anyone can re-derive it by hand.
Yours to keepValues, facts, and plans are files. It works offline and fits on a flash drive.
ForkableDon't like how it is run? Take the whole thing and run your own.

Promises are cheap, so here is one that costs something. Where the evidence is thin, we award nothing and say so in public: 312 measures are published as withheld, next to the 374 that earned a top mark. That list is the work we owe you, in the open, where leaving it undone has a price.
